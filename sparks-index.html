Who makes a good Spark

You may already be the person people ask.

  • “How did you do that?”
  • “Can you show me?”
  • Explain why. Let people try.
  • Welcome questions.
  • Learn from the room.

You don’t need every answer. You do need to care whether people understand.

What to expect

Small enough to ask. Practical enough to use.

Learn by doing

Live and virtual, 60 or 90 minutes

A learning goal and something to try or make.

Room for questions

Transcribed, not video-recorded

Bring the questions you’d hesitate to ask in a larger room.

Keep improving

Feedback from every session shapes the next.

Help shape the beginning

What would you like to pass on?

The form covers you, your workshop, who it helps, length, group size and availability. Have an example of your AI work ready: a demo, write-up or project, or a short description if it’s private.

The Education team reviews each proposal and, if it fits, works with you on the session, date and promotion. Sparks volunteer their time and are unpaid.
